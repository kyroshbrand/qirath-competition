Rules & Judging Criteria

👥

Age Groups & Categories

Participants are categorized based on their verified age as per their Aadhaar card:

  • Junior Category: Age 5 to 12 Years
  • Senior Category: Age 13 to 18 Years
  • Open Category: Age 19 Years and Above
🎙️

Recitation Video Guidelines

Follow these recording criteria to ensure your video submission qualifies:

  • Video length: 2 to 4 minutes of uninterrupted recitation
  • Face & mouth movements must be clearly visible
  • Clear audio without excessive background music or artificial echo
  • Recite any Surah / Ayah with proper Waqf and Tajweed
⚖️

Judging Parameters (100 Marks)

Evaluated by recognized Islamic scholars and Qaris:

  • Tajweed & Makharij: 40 Points
  • Husn al-Sawt (Voice & Tone): 30 Points
  • Waqf, Ibtida & Rhythm: 20 Points
  • Demeanor & Adab: 10 Points
